Our purpose

A club for our community

Prospector Soccer Club (PSC) serves Cameron Park, Shingle Springs and Rescue, California, in the El Dorado County foothills.

Our purpose is to lead in developing young talent and to administer and promote an organized, non-profit, volunteer soccer program for the children of our club’s territory.

Looking ahead

Room to grow

Our vision

To be a cornerstone of the community: developing players at every level while championing teamwork, respect, discipline, integrity and community.

Every player, recreational or competitive, should have room to grow and a lasting love of the game.

What brings us together

A rewarding place to play

Our mission

  • Give every player a supportive, rewarding place to play, focused on fun, enjoyment and skill at their level of interest and ability.
  • Create a challenging environment for players who want to reach their full potential.
  • Foster physical, mental and emotional growth through soccer at all ages and levels.
