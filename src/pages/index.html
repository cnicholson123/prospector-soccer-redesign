    Two ways to play

    Whether it’s a first touch on the ball or a shot at a tournament title, there’s a team at PSC for your player.

    Recreational · U6–U19

    Fun-first, local soccer with volunteer coaches. Players are placed on teams, practice once or twice a week, and play on Saturdays close to home.
