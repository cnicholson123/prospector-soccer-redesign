## Recreational Program (/rec)
- Ages U6 to U19.
- Training: practices typically begin mid-July; once or twice a week, set by the coach; duration set by the coach.
- Games: 8–10 games, typically starting mid-August; usually once a week on Saturdays; game length depends on age level.
- Registration cost:
  - April 1 – June 15: $170 U6–U12 (PreKinder–6th grade); $185 U14–U19 (7th–12th grade)
  - After June 15 (waitlist): $185 U6–U12; $195 U14–U19
- Equipment: uniform kit $55 (must be Capelli uniforms); shin guards; soccer cleats; water bottle; practice ball — size 3 for U6–U8, size 4 for U10–U12, size 5 for U14–U19.

- Competitive Coaches Wanted. Benefits: coaches choose their own roster; better practice fields (usually granted based on availability); opportunity to give back through player enrichment. New this year: partnership with The Coaching Manual for coach education and training; VEO cameras to record some home games; competition soccer balls; goalie training. Requirements: suggested Grassroots course for the age group; filled-out application. Button: 2026 Comp Coach Application (PDF) https://prospector.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/206/2026/01/2026-Comp-Coach-Application.pdf
- Cost: Registration $330 (once accepted onto a comp team, for the fall season). Suggested team fee $200 (covers at least two tournaments per year). Spring is a separate fee of $100–$150. Uniforms $160–$200 (usually last 2–3 years).
- FAQ:
  - Is comp year-round? Only depending on the coach. At minimum there is a fall season and two tournaments. Some coaches play indoor/futsal in winter; spring is optional.
  - How far do we travel? Mostly local (Sacramento, Folsom, El Dorado Hills), with some games in Roseville, Yuba City, Chico and Truckee. Not every division has teams in the farther locations.
- Sponsorship: PSC Comp Sponsorship Information (PDF) https://www.prospector.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/206/2023/08/PSC-Comp-Sponsorship-Information.pdf
- Program overview:
  - Team selection by tryouts in January, February or May depending on age group. Players must attend tryouts; a second team may form depending on tryout numbers and coach availability.
  - Teams train longer, usually play 4+ tournaments a year, and play cup games in addition to fall league.
  - Teams offered U9 through U19, boys and girls. 7v7 at U9, 9v9 at U11, 11v11 at U13 and older.
  - Head coaches are volunteers appointed through an application; some teams add professional coaches or trainers.
  - Coaches are encouraged to take U.S. Soccer Learning Center courses (https://learning.ussoccer.com/coach): introduction course is free; Grassroots courses are 2 hours and $25, reimbursed by the club once the certificate is received.
  - Season typically April through December, with options for spring and post-league cup play. Two or three 2-hour practices per week. Fall league is 10 games (5 home, 5 away), late August to mid-November.
  - Teams typically enter 4–5 tournaments, with at least one overnight travel tournament (e.g., Bay Area or Santa Cruz). Non-CYSA options include sand soccer and small-sided games.
  - League games are mostly in the Sacramento region, extending north to Chico and south to Modesto.

## Referees (/referees)
- The referee program builds a qualified, well-trained group of referees held to high standards, to provide a safe, enjoyable environment for players of all ages and skill levels. Referees should know and correctly apply the Laws of the Game. The club actively recruits, develops and advances referees at all levels — referees are critical to the club's success. ## About Us (/about)
- Areas served: Cameron Park, Shingle Springs and Rescue, California.
- Purpose: to lead in developing young talent and to administer and promote an organized, non-profit, volunteer soccer program for the children of the club's territory.
- Vision: to be a cornerstone of the community — developing players at every level while championing teamwork, respect, discipline, integrity and community, so that every player, rec or comp, has room to grow and a lasting love of the game.
- Mission: give every player a supportive, rewarding place to play, focused on fun, enjoyment and skill at their level of interest and ability; create a challenging environment for players who want to reach their full potential; foster physical, mental and emotional growth through soccer at all ages and levels.
- Core values: Teamwork (builds trust and communication — we win and lose together); Respect (for the game, teammates, opponents, coaches and officials); Discipline (following the rules and staying committed); Integrity (fair play, honesty and transparency); Community (building lasting relationships through soccer).
