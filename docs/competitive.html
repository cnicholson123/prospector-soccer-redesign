The competitive program

More time on the ball

The playing year

Typically April through December, with spring and post-league cup options. Two or three 2-hour practices each week.

Fall league

10 games: 5 home and 5 away, from late August to mid-November. Teams play cup games in addition to fall league.

Age groups

Boys and girls, U9 through U19. 7v7 at U9, 9v9 at U11, and 11v11 at U13 and older.

Coaching

Volunteer head coaches are appointed through an application. Some teams add professional coaches or trainers.

Finding your team

  • Team selection is by tryouts in January, February or May, depending on age group. Players must attend tryouts.
  • A second team may form depending on tryout numbers and coach availability.
  • Teams train longer and usually play 4+ tournaments a year.

Beyond the home field

  • Teams typically enter 4–5 tournaments, including at least one overnight travel tournament, such as the Bay Area or Santa Cruz.
  • Non-CYSA options include sand soccer and small-sided games.
  • League games are mostly in the Sacramento region, extending north to Chico and south to Modesto.

Good to know

Competitive questions

Is comp year-round?

Only depending on the coach. At minimum, there is a fall season and two tournaments. Some coaches play indoor/futsal in winter; spring is optional.

How far do we travel?

Mostly locally: Sacramento, Folsom and El Dorado Hills, with some games in Roseville, Yuba City, Chico and Truckee. Not every division has teams in the farther locations. League play can extend south to Modesto; teams typically also enter at least one overnight travel tournament.

How do tryouts work?

Players must attend tryouts, held in January, February or May depending on age group. A second team may form depending on tryout numbers and coach availability.

What is the team size by age?

Playing formats are 7v7 at U9, 9v9 at U11, and 11v11 at U13 and older.

Is coach education reimbursed?

The U.S. Soccer Learning Center introduction course is free. Grassroots courses are 2 hours and $25; the club reimburses the course fee once the certificate is received.
