Play with PSC

A season to grow

Recreational soccer for players from U6 to U19.

Prospector Soccer Club is a non-profit, 100% volunteer-run youth soccer club serving Cameron Park, Shingle Springs and Rescue in the El Dorado County foothills.

From practice to game day

Your season at a glance

Practices

Typically begin mid-July, once or twice a week. Your coach sets the schedule and practice duration.

Games

8–10 games, typically starting mid-August. Games are usually once a week on Saturdays; game length depends on age level.
