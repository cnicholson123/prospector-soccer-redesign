# Youth soccer club merchandise vendor research

## Method and date

Researched 2026-11-09, America/Los_Angeles. Public business websites, publicly served calculator code, search-index excerpts and visible third-party listings were read. No business was contacted, no form was submitted, and no quote request, account creation, signup, sample order or purchase was made.

The directory contains 14 businesses with public locations in El Dorado or Sacramento County. “Currently operating” means a live business site, contact/service information or recent public activity was observed. Operation was not confirmed by contact. Great Lapel Pins has weaker current-operation evidence and an address inconsistency, explicitly retained as a follow-up lead.

Pricing classes are **(a)** a published price/rate or public-calculator input, **(b)** a published starting-at price, and **(c)** no public dollar price found for the requested work. Derived arithmetic is an estimate, even when every input is class (a). A public minimum alone is not a dollar quote. All amounts are USD.

Search snippets can lag live pages. Live values take precedence where available. Some sites returned blocked pages or omitted widget selections. those gaps are named. Directory and business-controlled review widgets are attributed rather than treated as independently verified Google scores.

Product quantities refer to complete items. Product 7 means four-pin sets, and product 10 means decal-plus-magnet pairs. Adult standard apparel sizes are assumed for budgeting. the club serves youth, but no youth/adult size mix was supplied. A pickaxe is assumed at up to 4,000 stitches and a crest at up to 8,000. These are modeling choices, not digitized stitch counts.

## Gaps and recommended questions for Chris to ask later

1. **One comparable specification sheet.** Supply the crest and pickaxe as vector artwork, finished logo dimensions, desired thread/ink colors, adult/youth size mix, quantities by SKU and date needed. Ask each shop to itemize blanks, decoration, setup, tax, delivery and any spoilage allowance.
2. **Hoodie construction and screens.** Specify a navy 10-oz-class heavyweight or exact preferred SKU. Confirm whether four colors includes the underbase, whether sleeve embroidery requires a placement surcharge, and whether a small pickaxe stays within the modeled stitch band.
3. **Tee details.** Confirm solid royal, cotton content and youth availability. Ask RCD for the actual royal estimate because its quick calculator only exposed other dark colors. Ask whether sizes and styles can share a print run.
4. **Scarf sourcing.** Request genuine jacquard-knit yarn artwork with two sides and fringe, not surface printing on a generic scarf. Ask about minimum per design/colorway, knit setup, preproduction sample, importer/manufacturer location and delivery.
5. **Headwear.** Distinguish patch manufacture, merrow/laser-cut edge, attachment and cap cost. Specify woven versus embroidered crest, cuffed pom construction and youth/adult fit. Confirm that blank or direct-embroidery pricing has not been substituted for the patch-cap request.
6. **Drinkware.** R5 is the clearest in-county metal-bottle candidate. Ask for the 32-oz insulated blank SKU, engraved area, one or two sides, setup, and whether each player name adds cost. Branded and Stencil Stop need material/cylinder compatibility confirmed.
7. **Pin sets.** Ask Great Lapel Pins for four separate hard-enamel designs, 1–1.5 inches, quantities per design, tooling for each, number of colors, plating, posts/clutches, backing card, four-pin set assembly, shipping and tariff treatment. Verify its public address and present operation.
8. **Blanket and backpack access.** Require an embroidery zipper/access panel where needed, proof that the backpack has a separate cleat compartment and a youth-suitable size, and confirmation that supplied blanks are accepted. Ask about hooping, lining visibility and damage/replacement terms.
9. **Decal/magnet durability.** Ask for 5-inch outdoor UV-resistant laminated vinyl and 6×4-inch oval vehicle-grade magnet thickness, print, cutting and setup. The national 0.01-inch magnet benchmark requires a suitability check. Request exact 24/48/100 prices instead of area-scaling a sign price.
10. **Club-store operations.** Ask RCD, Bougie and Conquer about store setup/monthly fees, club margin, fulfillment per order, family shipping, pickup windows, reorder minimums, artwork ownership and digitizing reuse.

## Caveats

- Exact complete local quotes could not be verified from public information. The report intentionally leaves missing costs unknown. Published service capability and a live storefront are not a guarantee of capacity, inventory or continued operation.
- The national hoodie and blanket hybrid scenarios combine public blank and decoration prices across suppliers. Handling, freight between suppliers, garment markups and acceptance of supplied goods can change the result. The Athletico backpack scenario has an explicit stock uncertainty and should be used only as a budget reference.
- The headline local price table contains component subtotals. It is not a complete merchandise budget. No grand club-order total is calculated from incompatible partial prices, buy-up quantities and alternative specifications.
- Published screen fees are modeled per screen/color. Artwork is assumed production-ready. Actual stitch count, color separations, cap patch size, seam access and extra underbase requirements remain unverified. Each product is modeled as a separate order with its own setup. Cross-product digitizing reuse has not been assumed.
- All arithmetic excludes sales tax and unposted freight, rush, art cleanup, patch attachment, packaging and set assembly. A blank price and a bundled decorated price are kept distinct. Some national prices include shipping or setup, as explicitly stated.
- The RCD estimator was inspected as public JavaScript and recomputed locally. Its royal color was unavailable, and its headwear digitizing fee was unpriced. Its public default white tee at 24 is $6.84 + $4.75 = $11.59, total $278.16, a cross-check rather than the requested royal design. [RCD public estimator and contact](https://rcdincprintco.com/) [RCD publicly served calculator JavaScript](https://rcdincprintco.com/assets/index-Bm1Rqqjl.js)
- TreeCity leaves 100+ embroidery on a custom-quote basis. Hard Eight’s single-color starting price and Costello’s larger magnets do not establish prices for the requested products. Peak’s displayed sticker total has unverified active options.
- The Edge Print & Promotional and Kombat Ink were excluded from the in-county directory because public results locate them in West Sacramento, Yolo County. Roseville/Rocklin businesses were not included merely for serving Sacramento. No local physical hard-enamel factory was verified. Great Lapel Pins advertises factory-direct sourcing.
- Reviews are snapshots, not quality guarantees. Where no numeric rating/count could be verified, none was invented. Blank addresses and phones mean not publicly verified in this research, not that the business lacks them.
